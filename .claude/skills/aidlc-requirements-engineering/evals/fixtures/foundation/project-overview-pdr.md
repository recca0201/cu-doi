# Project overview

## Product name
FieldOps Inspector

## Personas
- Field Technician
- Operations Manager

## Product scope
- Mobile-first field inspection workflow
- Offline checklist completion during site visits
- Photo capture for defects and evidence
- Sync queued inspection results when connectivity returns

## Business goals
- Reduce time to complete on-site inspections
- Improve inspection data quality and photo evidence capture

## Out of scope
- Public marketing website
- Social community features
- Referral or affiliate programs
